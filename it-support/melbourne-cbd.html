IT support in Melbourne CBD: common questions

Can you work with our building's internet and network?

Yes. We regularly work with building-provided services and landlords' IT contacts to set up secure, separate networks for each tenant.

We're moving offices. When should we call you?

Ideally 2–3 months ahead. Internet services can take weeks to install, and cabling should be planned before fit-out. Our new office checklist on the free downloads page covers the timeline.
