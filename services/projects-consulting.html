Overview

Some work doesn't fit inside day-to-day support: a new building, a new school wing, a merger, a move to the cloud. We scope it clearly, agree a fixed plan and then deliver it with regular updates, testing and proper handover documentation.

What's included

  • IT strategy and three-year technology roadmaps
  • Office and campus moves, fit-outs and new sites
  • Cloud, email and tenant-to-tenant migrations
  • Windows 11 and fleet upgrades
  • IT audits and health checks
  • Project management with weekly status updates
