FAQ

Common questions

What is the Essential Eight?

Eight mitigation strategies published by the Australian Cyber Security Centre, such as MFA, patching and backups. Together they stop most common attacks.

Will this help with our cyber insurance?

Usually, yes. Insurers ask about MFA, backups, EDR and training. We help you answer their questionnaire accurately and close the gaps they care about.

We think we have been hacked. What now?

Call us straight away on the number at the top of the page and see the Cyber incident page for the first steps to take.
