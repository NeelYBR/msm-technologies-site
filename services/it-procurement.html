Overview

Buying IT is easy to get wrong: the wrong spec, the wrong warranty, licences you don't need. We recommend what fits, source it from trusted Australian distributors, set it up and deliver it ready for your people. Just tell us what you need and we'll come back with a written quote.

What's included

  • Laptops, desktops, Macs, iPads and Chromebooks
  • Network gear: switches, access points and firewalls
  • Microsoft 365, Adobe and other software licensing
  • Zero-touch enrolment and imaging before delivery
  • Asset tagging and an up-to-date asset register
  • Warranty claims and secure e-waste recycling
