Cyber incident response

Think you've been hacked? Call us now.

Ransomware, a compromised mailbox, a fake invoice that got paid: the first hour matters. We'll help you contain it, recover and work out what happened.

Call 0449 853 637 nowSend us the details

Right now

What to do in the next 15 minutes

  1. Call us on the number above. Don't wait for email.
  2. Disconnect affected computers from the network (unplug the cable or turn off Wi-Fi). Don't switch them off: evidence lives in memory.
  3. Don't pay a ransom or reply to the attacker.
  4. If an email account is involved, change its password from a different, clean device and check for new forwarding rules.
  5. If money may have been sent, call your bank straight away.
  6. Write down what happened and when: what you saw, clicked or received.

Signs of an incident

  • Files renamed or won't open, ransom notes on screen
  • Colleagues receiving strange emails "from you"
  • Unexpected MFA prompts or password reset emails
  • Bank details changed on an invoice
  • Antivirus alerts or very slow computers

Our incident response process

  1. 1

    Contain

    Isolate affected devices and accounts to stop the spread.

  2. 2

    Investigate

    Find out what happened, how they got in, and what was touched.
