Our promise

Seven promises we put in writing

Changing IT provider is a leap of faith. These promises are how we make it a small one.

  • No lock-in contracts
  • 15-minute response target
  • First-month satisfaction guarantee
  • Plain-English invoices
  • No surprise fees
  • Your data stays in Australia
  • You own your passwords and documentation
1

No lock-in contracts

Month-to-month once you are onboarded.

After onboarding, our managed services run month to month. If we stop earning your business, you can leave with 30 days notice. We would rather keep you because the service is good than because a contract says so.

  • Month-to-month after onboarding
  • 30 days notice to leave, no exit fees
  • Hardware and project work quoted separately, so nothing is bundled to trap you
2

15-minute response target

For critical issues during business hours.

When something critical breaks, like a whole site down or a suspected cyber attack, we aim to respond within 15 minutes during business hours. You will get a real engineer, and you will know what is happening and when.

  • Critical (P1) issues: 15-minute response target
  • Clear priority levels so you know what to expect
  • Response times reported back to you every quarter
3

First-month satisfaction guarantee

Not happy in month one? Walk away.

If you are not happy with our support during your first full month of managed services, tell us. We'll try to put it right, and if you still want to leave we'll waive that month's managed service fee and help hand everything over.

  • Covers the first full month of managed services
  • We hand over all documentation if you leave
  • No awkward conversations, just tell us
4

Plain-English invoices

Every line explained, no jargon.

Your invoice should make sense without a phone call. Every line says what it is, how many, and what it covers, and licences are listed so you can see exactly what you are using.

  • Itemised by service, licence and user
  • Changes from last month highlighted
  • One invoice, one date, every month
5

No surprise fees

Anything outside your plan is quoted first.

If a job falls outside your agreement, we tell you before we start and give you a written quote to approve. You will never find a mystery charge for "after-hours admin" or "travel" you did not agree to.

  • Out-of-scope work quoted and approved first
  • No hidden call-out, travel or admin charges
  • Clear list of what is and isn't included
6

Your data stays in Australia

Backups and tools hosted onshore wherever possible.

We choose Australian data centres for backups, monitoring and documentation tools wherever the option exists, and we tell you upfront about any service that stores data offshore so you can decide.

  • Australian regions chosen for backups and cloud services
  • Any offshore storage disclosed in writing
  • Aligned with the Australian Privacy Principles
7

You own your passwords and documentation

Your systems, your keys, always.

Admin passwords, licences, domain names and documentation belong to you. You can ask for a full copy at any time, and if you ever leave, we hand everything over in an organised, usable form.

  • Admin accounts and domains registered to your organisation
  • Full documentation available on request
  • A clean, organised handover if you ever move on

Questions

About our promises

Is there really no minimum contract?

Managed services are month to month after onboarding. Onboarding itself is a one-off piece of work, agreed upfront, and some licences (like Microsoft annual plans) have their own terms, which we explain before you commit.

What counts as a "critical" issue?

Something that stops a whole site or team from working, or a suspected security incident. Day-to-day requests have their own response targets, which you can see on our How we work page.

How does the first-month guarantee work?

Tell us within your first full month of managed services that you're not happy. We'll try to fix it; if you still want to leave, we waive that month's managed service fee and hand over your documentation.

Can I get a copy of my passwords and documentation?

Yes, any time. They belong to you. We keep them in a secure documentation system and can export them for you.
