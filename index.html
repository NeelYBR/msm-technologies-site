FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't see yours? Ask us directly.

What is a managed service provider (MSP)?

An MSP looks after your IT for a predictable monthly fee. Instead of calling someone only when things break, we monitor, patch, secure and support your systems continuously, so problems are caught early and your team always has someone to call.

How much does it cost?

Every organisation is different, so we quote each one individually. Use our Get a quote form to tell us what you need and we'll send a clear, tailored, no-obligation quote within 1 business day.

What happens after I request a quote?

An MSM engineer reviews your selections and contacts you at the time that suits you, usually within 1 business day. We'll ask a few questions if needed, then send a clear written quote. There's no obligation.

Do you support both Windows and Mac?

Yes. We support Windows, macOS, iPadOS, ChromeOS and the cloud platforms behind them, including Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Intune and Jamf.

Do you work with schools?

Absolutely, education is one of our specialities, and our team has run IT for schools with 1,000+ devices. We understand device rollouts, student and staff accounts, filtering, timetabled demand and the realities of supporting a busy campus.

Can you work alongside our internal IT person or team?

Yes. Our co-managed support gives your in-house staff extra hands, tools, security monitoring and escalation, while they stay in control.

Do you offer onsite support?

Most issues are solved remotely within minutes. When something needs hands on the ground, we come onsite across metropolitan Melbourne.

Are we locked into a long contract?

We'd rather earn your business every month. Talk to us about flexible terms that suit your organisation.

What if we've been hit by a cyber attack?

Call us straight away. We'll help you contain the incident, restore systems from backup, and put protections in place so it doesn't happen again.
