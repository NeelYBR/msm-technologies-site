MSM vs hiring in-house IT

An honest side-by-side for schools, small businesses and not-for-profits deciding how to look after their IT. Both can work well. Here's where each shines.

Managed IT with MSM compared with an in-house IT employee
TopicManaged IT with MSMIn-house IT person
CostUsually MSMOne predictable monthly fee that grows or shrinks with your team. No recruitment, training or tool costs on top.Salary plus super, leave, training, recruitment and the tools they need. Usually the larger commitment for a small team.
Cover when someone is awayUsually MSMA whole team behind you. Leave, sick days and resignations don't stop support.One or two people. When they are away or leave, knowledge often walks out with them.
Range of skillsUsually MSMSpecialists in networks, cyber, Microsoft 365, Apple and servers, available when needed.Strong generalists, but no one person can be expert in everything.
Security tools & monitoringUsually MSMBusiness-grade monitoring, EDR and backup tools included, watched by the team.Tools bought and configured separately, and monitored only when the person is on shift.
Being on site every dayUsually in-houseRemote first, with scheduled on-site days and fast call-outs when hands are needed.Always in the building, able to walk over and help in seconds.
Knowing your people and cultureUsually in-houseA named engineer who gets to know you, plus documented processes.Deep day-to-day knowledge of your people, history and quirks.
DocumentationUsually MSMEverything documented in a shared system you own and can access any time.Often lives in one person's head or a notebook, unless time is set aside for it.
Scaling up or downUsually MSMAdd users, sites or services in days. Scale back without redundancies.Growth means hiring; quieter times mean paying for unused capacity.
Strategy & planningDependsQuarterly reviews, roadmaps and budgets drawn from what works across many clients.Depends on the person's seniority and how much time firefighting leaves.

Choose managed IT if…

You have fewer than about 100 staff, nobody wants to be the "IT person", you need security done properly, or you want predictable costs and cover all year round.

Choose in-house if…

You're large enough to keep a team busy full-time, need someone physically present all day, or run very specialised systems only an insider can learn.

Keep your in-house person and add our tools, specialists and after-hours cover. See how co-managed IT works.

Questions

Common questions

Is an MSP always cheaper than hiring?

Not always. For most organisations under about 100 staff, a managed service costs less than a full-time IT person once on-costs and tools are included, but a larger organisation with complex needs may justify its own team. We'll tell you honestly which fits.

We already have an IT person. Do we have to choose?

No. Co-managed IT keeps your in-house person and gives them our tools, specialists, after-hours cover and a backstop for leave. Many schools and growing businesses work this way.

What happens to our IT person if we switch to MSM?

That's your decision. Often they move into a role closer to the business, like projects, training or systems, while we handle the day-to-day support and monitoring.
